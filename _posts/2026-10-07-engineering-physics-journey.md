# My Engineering Physics Journey

## Why Engineering Physics?

Engineering Physics combines the mathematical and scientific foundations of physics with the practical problem-solving approach of engineering. That combination is what initially attracted me to the field.

I am interested in understanding not only how physical systems work, but also how those principles can be used to design, improve, and build real engineering systems.

My interests include aerospace engineering, mechanical systems, advanced manufacturing, experimental physics, and emerging technologies.

## Connecting Physics and Engineering

One of the aspects of Engineering Physics that I find most valuable is the ability to approach a problem from multiple perspectives.

Physics provides the foundation for understanding forces, energy, motion, thermodynamics, and other physical phenomena. Engineering then applies that understanding to practical systems and design challenges.

This combination is particularly important when working with complex systems where performance, reliability, efficiency, and manufacturability all need to be considered.

## My Engineering Interests

I am particularly interested in:

- Aerospace systems
- Mechanical design
- Advanced manufacturing
- Propulsion and high-performance systems
- Experimental measurement
- Engineering analysis
- Artificial intelligence and emerging engineering technologies
- Construction

I am especially interested in how new computational tools and artificial intelligence can be integrated into engineering workflows while maintaining sound physical and engineering principles.

## Building Experience Through Projects

My coursework and laboratory projects have given me experience with experimental measurement, data analysis, mathematical modeling, thermodynamics, mechanics, programming, and technical communication.

Projects such as my reversible pendulum experiment have allowed me to apply theoretical concepts to real measurements and evaluate experimental uncertainty.

These experiences have reinforced my interest in engineering problems that require both analytical reasoning and practical experimentation.

## Looking Toward the Future

My long-term goal is to work in advanced engineering and manufacturing, particularly in industries involved with aerospace and high-performance systems.

I want to contribute to the design and improvement of systems where physics, engineering, manufacturing, and emerging technologies intersect.

Ultimately, I hope to use my engineering education and experience to develop innovative technologies and eventually create engineering solutions of my own.

## Continuing to Develop

Engineering is a field that requires continuous learning. I plan to continue developing my skills in engineering analysis, computer-aided design, programming, experimental methods, and advanced manufacturing.

This portfolio will document that progress through my academic projects, experiments, and engineering work.
